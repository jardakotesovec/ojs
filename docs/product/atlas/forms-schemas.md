## Gaps
- Scope excluded `Field*.php` widget classes (`FieldText`, `FieldSelect`, `FieldUpload`, `FieldAuthors`, `FieldCreditRoles`, `FieldOrcid`, `FieldPubId`, etc. — ~29 in lib/pkp, 2 in OJS root) and the `Field`/`FormComponent` base classes themselves, since they are reusable field widgets, not standalone forms tied to a settings page/entity.
